"Launch All" starts Ollama then AUTOMATIC1111 in sequence, streaming each to the terminal panel below. Ollama usually auto-starts as a Windows service already, so this button mainly matters for AUTOMATIC1111. "Kill conflicting process" checks ports 7860/7861/7862 for a leftover AUTOMATIC1111 process (e.g. from a crashed run) and force-kills it after you confirm.

Book Structure

Length is best increased by adding chapters, not by raising words per chapter. On real runs, chapters past ~3,000 words are where repetition collapse starts showing up — a 4,357-word chapter measured 0.357 against the 0.36 floor, while 1,200–2,400-word chapters held 0.51–0.59.
